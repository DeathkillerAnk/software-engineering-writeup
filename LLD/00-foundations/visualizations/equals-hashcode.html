How a broken hashCode loses your key in a HashMap

LLD · 00-foundations — put a key, then get an equal key. Toggle the hashCode and watch the difference.

Both p1 and p2 are new Point(1,2) — equal by value. Press put.
The contract: if a.equals(b) then a.hashCode() == b.hashCode() — required. A HashMap finds the bucket with hashCode() first, and only compares with equals() inside that bucket. Break the contract and equal keys scatter to different buckets, so get never even reaches the bucket where put left the entry → null, though the key is right there. Fix: a record generates a correct hashCode for free.
