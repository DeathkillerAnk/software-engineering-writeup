The core depends on its ports (interfaces). Adapters depend on the core, never the reverse.
Takeaway: dependencies point inward. The core owns the UseCase and Repository interfaces; every adapter implements/consumes them. Swapping Postgres → DynamoDB, or REST → CLI, is a new adapter — the business rules don't change. That's the Dependency Rule, and it's what makes the core testable with an in-memory adapter (the src/ drill).
