The abstraction sweet spot

LLD · 06 — drag the slider. Both too little and too much abstraction raise the cost to change. Principals aim for the dip.

0 — none (copy-paste everywhere)sweet spot100 — everything abstracted
Takeaway: abstraction is a loan against complexity — paid now in indirection for a benefit that may never arrive. Under-abstract and every change means editing ten copies; over-abstract and every change means navigating a maze. The dip isn't a fixed point — it's "abstract exactly where a second reason to change has already shown up."
