Token Bucket vs Leaky Bucket

Two classic rate-limiting algorithms, side by side. The token bucket lets traffic burst up to a stored capacity, then settles to a steady refill rate. The leaky bucket queues everything and drains at one strictly constant rate. Watch a burst of requests hit each and see which gets through.
