Circuit Breaker State Machine

A caller sends requests through a circuit breaker to a flaky downstream service. Watch how the breaker trips OPEN after too many failures to fail fast and shield the downstream, then probes with HALF-OPEN before recovering to CLOSED.

4s
Ready. The breaker is CLOSED. Press Step to send one request, or Play to stream requests.

Legend

CLOSED — traffic flows, failures counted
OPEN — fail fast, downstream shielded
HALF-OPEN — one trial request allowed
Request packet in flight
Fallback / immediate error to caller

Try this

Set the downstream to failing and press Play. After the threshold is hit the breaker trips OPEN — notice the calls received counter on the downstream stops increasing: it is shielded. Now flip the downstream back to healthy and wait for the cooldown: the HALF-OPEN trial request succeeds and the breaker closes again.
