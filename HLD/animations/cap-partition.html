CAP: Choosing C vs A During a Partition

A 3-replica cluster serves reads and writes. While the network is healthy, it is both Consistent and Available at once. CAP only forces a choice during a network partition — CP rejects writes on the cut-off side to stay consistent; AP keeps accepting writes everywhere and diverges, then must reconcile a conflict when the network heals.

Cluster healthy. All replicas agree on x = 1. Both Consistency and Availability hold — CAP says nothing here.

Try this: Inject the partition once in CP mode and watch the minority node refuse a write to protect consistency. Then Reset, switch to AP, inject again, and let both sides accept writes — when you Heal, the two values collide and must be reconciled. The trade-off you just felt did not exist before the partition.
