LSM-Tree vs B-Tree

Two storage engines, two philosophies. LSM-Trees batch writes in memory and append them sequentially, paying later with background compaction (write amplification). B-Trees update pages in place along one path, keeping reads cheap. Switch modes and write keys to watch each strategy pay its own bill.
