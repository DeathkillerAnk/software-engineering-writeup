Leader-Follower Replication & Failover

A client writes to one leader, which replicates each write to two followers. Watch how the sync vs async choice and replication lag decide whether an acknowledged write survives when the leader suddenly dies.

Try this: Set lag to max, keep ASYNC, send 4 quick writes, then Kill leader mid-flight. Watch acknowledged (amber) writes turn red — they are gone. Now flip to SYNC and repeat: the client waits longer, but every acked write survives because the leader only acks after a follower has it.
