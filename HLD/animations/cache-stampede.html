Cache Stampede & Mitigation

A hot cache key expires. Without protection, every concurrent client misses at once and all of them hammer the slow database — a thundering herd. Toggle mitigations and watch the database QPS meter spike vs. stay flat.

Press Play or Step expiry to watch what happens when the TTL hits zero.

What you're seeing

The green ring is the key's TTL counting down. When it hits zero the key is stale, so the next reads miss.

Single-flight: only one client (the leader) recomputes; the rest wait and reuse its result → exactly 1 DB query.

Jitter + early recompute: each client refreshes a bit before expiry at a random offset, so misses spread out instead of synchronizing.

Try this: set clients to the max with both mitigations OFF and Step the expiry — watch QPS spike to the client count. Now turn Single-flight ON and Step again: peak QPS drops to exactly 1, no matter how many clients.
