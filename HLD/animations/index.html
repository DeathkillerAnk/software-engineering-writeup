▶ HLD Interactive Animations

Twenty self-contained, offline visualizations for the hardest-to-picture ideas in this curriculum. Each opens in your browser with no install or internet — play, step, drag, and break them.

← Back to the curriculum index
How to use these. Don't just watch — predict first. Before you press play, guess what will happen; before you remove a node or inject a partition, guess the outcome. The gap between your guess and what the animation does is exactly where the learning is. Each animation links to its companion writeup at the bottom.

Storage & data structures

storage-engines

▶ LSM-Tree vs B-Tree

Watch writes flow into a memtable, flush to SSTables, and get rewritten by compaction (write amplification) — versus a B-tree's in-place updates. See why one is write-optimized and the other read-optimized.
storage-engines

▶ Bloom Filter

Insert items, set bits with k hashes, and watch a false positive appear — while seeing why false negatives are impossible. The probabilistic membership test behind every LSM read.

Scaling & distribution

load-balancing

▶ Consistent Hashing

Add and remove nodes on the ring and watch only ~1/N of keys remap — then hit the naive mod-N button and watch almost everything reshuffle. Toggle virtual nodes.
load-balancing

▶ Load Balancing Algorithms

Round-robin vs least-connections vs power-of-two-choices, with live load bars. See how two random probes get you a near-flat distribution with no global state.
caching

▶ Cache Stampede & Mitigation

A hot key's TTL expires and every client hammers the DB at once. Toggle single-flight and jitter and watch the DB-load spike collapse to a single fetch.
partitioning

▶ Partitioning & Rebalancing

Spread data across shards, add a node and watch a slice rebalance, and flip on a hot key to see one shard melt. Hash vs range partitioning, and the timestamp hotspot.

Replication & consistency

replication

▶ Replication & Failover

Writes flow leader → followers with adjustable lag. Kill the leader under async replication and watch acknowledged-but-unreplicated writes vanish. Compare sync.
replication

▶ Quorum Reads & Writes

Drag R, W, and N. When R + W > N the read and write quorums must overlap — watch the intersecting node guarantee a fresh read, and watch stale reads appear when they don't.
consistency

▶ CAP: C vs A in a Partition

Inject a network partition and choose: CP (the minority rejects writes, going unavailable) or AP (both sides accept and diverge, then reconcile). The trade-off exists only during the partition.
transactions

▶ Isolation Anomalies & MVCC

Step two transactions through a lost update and a write skew. Switch to snapshot isolation (write skew survives!) and serializable (one aborts). The subtlety that trips everyone up.
time-ordering

▶ Vector Clocks & Causality

Fire events and pass messages across three processes; pick any two events and learn whether one happened-before the other or they're concurrent — the thing Lamport clocks can't tell you.

Coordination & consensus

consensus

▶ Raft: Election & Replication

Five nodes, randomized timeouts, terms, and votes. Watch a candidate win a majority, replicate its log, then kill the leader and watch a fresh election. The marquee one.
transactions

▶ 2PC vs Saga

Crash the coordinator mid-2PC and watch participants block forever holding locks. Then run a saga, fail a step, and watch compensations unwind in reverse — with no isolation.

Reliability

reliability

▶ Circuit Breaker

Closed → Open → Half-Open. Make the downstream fail, watch the breaker trip and fail fast (shielding the downstream), then probe and recover.
reliability

▶ Retry Storms: Backoff + Jitter

An outage ends and everyone retries at once, re-toppling the service in synchronized waves. Turn on exponential backoff with full jitter and watch the load smooth out.

Messaging, rate limiting & app patterns

messaging

▶ Log vs Queue

A queue hands each message to one consumer and deletes it; a partitioned log retains messages and lets independent consumer groups read at their own offsets and replay. Order is per-partition only.
rate-limiting

▶ Token vs Leaky Bucket

Tokens drip in up to a capacity; a burst drains them, then throttles to the refill rate. Flip to a leaky bucket and watch a strictly constant output rate instead.
rate-limiting

▶ Rate-Limiting Windows

Cluster requests around a window boundary and watch a fixed window let through 2× the limit — then watch a sliding window correctly reject them.
news-feed

▶ Feed Fan-out: Push vs Pull

Post as a normal user (push to followers) then as a celebrity (write explosion!). Switch to pull (cheap writes, costly reads) and see why real systems go hybrid.
geo-search

▶ Spatial Indexing: Quadtree

Scatter points and watch cells subdivide where it's dense. Run a "find nearby" query that touches a handful of cells instead of scanning everything. Compare a naive uniform grid.
