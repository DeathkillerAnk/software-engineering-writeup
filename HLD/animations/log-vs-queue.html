Log vs Queue (Kafka vs SQS)

A queue hands each message to one competing consumer and deletes it on ack — no replay. A partitioned log appends messages and keeps them; each consumer group tracks its own offset, can rewind to replay, and order is guaranteed only within a partition.

QUEUE — competing consumers, delete on ack

LOG — partitioned, retained, per-group offsets
