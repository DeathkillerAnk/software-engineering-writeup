Feed Fan-out: Push vs Pull

How does a social network build your timeline? Either it pushes each new post into every follower's inbox when it's written, or it pulls posts together from everyone you follow when you open the app. Watch what happens when a celebrity with thousands of followers hits "post".

Pick a mode and press “Post →” (or Step) to see how the feed is built.
