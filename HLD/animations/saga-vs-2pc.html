Distributed Transactions: 2PC vs Saga

Two ways to make several services agree on one outcome. 2PC locks everyone and votes, but a crashed coordinator blocks participants forever. A Saga never locks, running compensations to undo on failure, but its intermediate states are visible to others.

click Play to run, Step to advance one event
Pick a mode and press Play. In 2PC the coordinator asks everyone to prepare; in a Saga each step commits locally and is undone by a compensation on failure.
