Raft: Leader Election & Log Replication

Five servers keep a replicated log in sync. They elect a single leader by majority vote, then the leader replicates client commands; an entry only commits once a majority has stored it. Kill the leader and watch the cluster heal itself.

Legend

Follower — obeys the current leader
Candidate — ran out its timeout, requesting votes
Leader — won a majority for this term
Dead — crashed / killed
Replicated, not yet committed
Committed (stored on a majority)
Open dot ring = current vote / election progress

Try this

Press Add client command a few times and watch boxes turn from blue to purple as a majority stores them. Then hit Kill leader: surviving nodes time out, one becomes a candidate in a higher term, collects 3 votes, and resumes replication — no committed entry is ever lost. Slow the Speed slider down to read every step.
